# Student Finance Analyzer

## Mission
Build an educational tool that helps students understand company
financial information without requiring a background in finance.

## Target Users
High school and college students who are interested in finance
but don't know how to analyze a company.

## Core Features

### 1. Company Search
Users can search for a publicly traded company.

### 2. Financial Overview
Display important metrics such as:
- Revenue
- Net income
- Cash
- Debt
- Free cash flow
- Revenue growth
- Profit margin

### 3. Valuation
Display educational valuation metrics such as:
- P/E
- P/S
- EV/EBITDA

### 4. Company Comparison
Allow users to compare a company with competitors.

### 5. AI Explanation
Use AI to explain the financial information in simple language.

The AI should:
- Explain financial terms
- Identify notable trends
- Explain potential strengths and weaknesses
- Suggest questions for further research

The AI should NOT:
- Tell users to buy or sell a stock
- Give personalized financial advice
- Predict stock prices

## User Testing

The first testing group will be students at Normandale Community College.

Goals:
- Get at least 25 student users
- Collect at least 10 feedback responses
- Identify confusing features
- Improve the product based on feedback

## Success

The project will be considered successful if students can use the
tool to better understand basic company financial information.
